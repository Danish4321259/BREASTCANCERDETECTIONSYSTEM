Clinical Metric Priority: Sensitivity (Recall for Malignant Cases)

In oncology screening, False Negatives (FN) represent malignant cancers mistakenly diagnosed as benign, resulting in delayed staging and poor prognosis. False Positives (FP) trigger confirmatory needle biopsies. Therefore, our model selection criteria strictly penalize false negatives by weighting Sensitivity (Recall) and ROC-AUC highest.
